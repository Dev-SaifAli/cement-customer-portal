Business rules & notes

Specification notes for the VAS Contract & Delivery portal. These describe automation, physical-site and driver-experience rules that govern how the operational screens must behave.

A · List-price change closes related contracts

Pricing

When a product's list price is changed, every contract related to that product must be automatically closed. A new price only applies through a brand-new contract created against the updated list.

EffectNo live contract may keep an old list price after the list changes — the system closes them so re-pricing is explicit and auditable.

B · Automatic fleet & loading control

Automation

Fleet management and Loading control run automatically, driven by:

  • the live truck status (waiting / at gate / loading / dispatched),
  • whether the truck is inside the site geofence (plant parking area),
  • and the silos & bagging-line capacity and rules.
Example
If PPC has a maximum of 4 trucks in the silo, once a truck exits the plant the first truck that arrived (and is waiting inside the geofence) automatically receives a notification to enter the plant for loading.

C · Physical location & silo numbering signs

Site

The plant must carry physical location signs and silo / line numbering signs that match the numbers used in the system, so a driver can visually find the exact silo or bagging line assigned to their load.

D · Full loading information to the driver

Driver app

Each driver must receive the complete loading information on their device:

  • loading schedule (date & time slot),
  • product to be loaded,
  • gate QR code,
  • and the assigned silo or bagging-line number.

F · Dispatch responsibilities

Dispatch

Dispatch must be able to bypass the automatic loading system and manually notify a driver to enter the plant. When dispatch does not intervene, the system performs this notification automatically:

  • based on the available capacity for each product (silo / bagging-line max trucks), and
  • by the first truck to arrive in the parking area (inside the plant geofence).
ModeAuto by default — capacity-driven, first-arrived-first-notified. Dispatch can switch to manual bypass at any time to call a specific driver in.

Delivery requests

Incoming delivery requests to schedule. A request may be assigned as one shipment or split into as many shipment lines as required. Split shipments use the parent number plus _S01, _S02, _S03… and each line has its own quantity, transporter, truck, driver and schedule.
